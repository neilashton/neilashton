# Neil Ashton

I am Vice President, Computational Engineering at Mistral, working at the intersection of
computational engineering and artificial intelligence. My research spans
high-fidelity computational fluid dynamics, scientific machine learning,
open datasets, foundation models for physical systems, and reliable agentic
systems for engineering design and analysis. Previously, I was a Distinguished Engineer at NVIDIA.

## Selected public work

- **[CAE ML Datasets](https://caemldatasets.org/)** — Open, high-fidelity
  AhmedML, WindsorML, DrivAerML and HiLiftAeroML datasets for reproducible
  automotive and aerospace research.
  [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/neashton)

- **[Fluid Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20455)** — A research
  perspective and scaling analysis for foundation models in computational
  fluid dynamics.
  [Reproducibility code](https://github.com/neilashton/fluid-intelligence-cfd-scaling)

- **[Automotive CFD Prediction Workshop](https://autocfd.org/)** — An
  international community benchmarking and validation programme for
  road-vehicle aerodynamics.

- **[Agentic computational engineering](https://www.nas.nasa.gov/pubs/ams/2026/04-09-26.html)**
  — Research on verifiable, traceable, tool-using AI systems with appropriate
  human oversight.
